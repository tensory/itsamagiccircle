Magic Circle Crochet

Magic Circle Crochet is an iOS app for crocheters. The app helps you plan and keep track of your work on flat circular patterns. Magic Circle Crochet can help when you’re making amigurumi, crocheted accessories, and more.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

This policy explains what the app does with data, which in short is nothing beyond what your device needs to run it.

Magic Circle Crochet does not collect or share any personal information. Any data you enter or modify stays on your device.

Your data

Patterns that you modify or create, and in-app settings, are stored only on your device. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Magic Circle Crochet patterns and in-app settings are not synced through iCloud. If you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your device backup along with your other app data.

Deleting the app deletes your patterns and settings. Your Premium Unlock purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored if you install the app again.

Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, photos, location, contacts, or notifications.

Purchases

The optional Premium Unlock is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, payment details, or Apple Account information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising networks. If you share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash and usage reports. You can turn this off in your iPhone’s Settings.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, this page will be updated, along with the date at the top.
