Magic Circle Crochet

Magic Circle Crochet is an iOS app for crocheters. The app helps you plan and keep track of your work on flat circular patterns. Magic Circle can help when you're making amigurumi, crocheted accessories, and more.

Contact Information

The developer of Magic Circle Crochet is Ari Lacenski.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Data Collection

Magic Circle does not collect or transmit any personal data, user information, or analytics from you.

Local Storage

Any data you create or settings that you select within Magic Circle Crochet are stored exclusively on your device. The app does not ask you to create an account, and there is no server side. No information about data or settings created in the app is transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Magic Circle Crochet does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app complies fully with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and related standards.

Changes to This Policy

The developer may update this privacy policy with respect to future app versions. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
